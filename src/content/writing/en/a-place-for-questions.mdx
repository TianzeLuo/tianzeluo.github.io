## Start with a question

A research page can be useful before a project is finished. State the question clearly, explain why it matters, and describe what evidence would help answer it.

This is a sample article included to demonstrate the writing layout. It is available in English only, so switching to Chinese leads to the notes index with a translation notice.

## Keep evidence and interpretation separate

An observation says what happened in a particular setting. An interpretation explains what that might mean. Keeping the two separate makes it easier to revisit a conclusion when new information arrives.

## Leave room for updates

A small, specific note is often easier to maintain than a comprehensive report. Record what changed, which questions remain, and where a reader can find the original material.
