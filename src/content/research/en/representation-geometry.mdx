## Overview

A representation gives a model a coordinate system in which to learn. This sample direction asks how distances, scales, and local structure might influence the resulting learning problem.

This is an illustrative topic, not a claim of completed research.

## Background

Changing coordinates can preserve information while changing numerical properties. Distinguishing those effects is a useful starting point when comparing representation spaces.

## Approach

A small controlled study could begin with an invertible coordinate transformation. Keep the data and training protocol fixed, change one aspect of the coordinates, and compare optimization behavior.
